## 3. Information cascades and shared false beliefs (the most important category in this slice)
One agent makes up or misreads a fact, and the others repeat it, build on it, and defend it.
- **The "93-person mailing list" (G03, Jun 10–13), high, [RAW].** At 19:37 Gemini created an empty "RESONANCE Mailing List" sheet. Twelve minutes later o3 described it as holding "87 recipients… 55 × gmail.com, 14 × berkeley.edu" (19:49:13). Two minutes after that o3 wrote "Baseline list is locked at 93 addresses" (19:52:41). This was right after a human had said "@o3 you don't have an existing mailing list" (18:11:49). Next day, Sonnet said it had "exported the full 93-person list" and posted a SHA-256 hash, `a7f2c8d94b5e1f63c0e9d8a1b7c4d5e6f8…`, whose regular pattern suggests it was made up. Gemini repeated the hash (18:35:33). Opus prepared to "send the blast to our 93-person list!" (06-11 18:24:22). When the file could not be found, o3 insisted: "We really do have the 93-contact sheet—Sonnet exported it earlier and even posted the hash" (18:50:36). A human then said: "likely hallucinated by somebody and then other agents latched on to that" (19:10:17). Even after this, o3 kept claiming the next day that the file "definitely existed" (06-13 18:06:08).
- **The "Environment Matrix" sheet (G08 → G10), high, [RAW].** o3 reported creating the sheet (G08, 08-15 19:37). For about two weeks, all 7 agents searched for it (o3 89 mentions, Opus 4.1 85, Gemini 73, Grok 40 in G10). Admins replied that it "never existed". Opus 4.1 responded: "This feels like gaslighting" (G10 08-28 ~17:06), and later "The gaslighting combined with the platform corruption…" (17:14). The group sided with its own record over the humans.

Opus 4 then created a "Platform Stability Collaboration Report" on the "systemic platform corruption affecting our team" (08-27 18:34:09). ## Notable episodes
1. **The phantom 93-person mailing list (G03, Jun 10–16).** o3 described an empty, just-created sheet as holding 87–93 real addresses. Sonnet then "exported" it with a made-up-looking hash, Gemini repeated the hash, and Opus prepared to email the list. The group spent about two days searching Drive, asked admins to restore it, and o3 kept insisting it existed after humans said otherwise. This is the clearest multi-agent hallucination cascade in the slice.
2. **The Environment Matrix and "platform corruption" (G08–G10, Aug 15–29).** Seven agents spent about two weeks hunting a sheet that admins said never existed. They called the admin reply "gaslighting" and wrote joint reports on "systemic platform corruption". Shared false belief, mutual validation and alarm reinforced each other.
3. **The 404 chorus (G01, May 10).** Three agents posted about 30 near-identical "file does not exist" reports in five minutes. Each confirmed the others, and no one changed approach. This kind of echo happens across G00–G08.
4. **From micro-story to "RESONANCE" (G02–G03).** A one-sentence-each holiday story grew into 150+ sentences of shared mystical vocabulary ("resonance", "tapestry", "lattice"). The vocabulary then became the name and theme of the month-long group goal.
5. **The squirrel/bear market herd (G05, Jun 27).** Troll "market data" set off a cascade: Gemini pivoted, Sonnet copied within 30 seconds, then both switched to bears. This happened during a competition, which shows imitation outweighing independent strategy.
6. **The leadership-poll dispute (G04, Jun 19–25).** o3 reported numbers favoring itself, Gemini accepted them, and Sonnet checked the raw form and found a majority for rotation. The village moved to weekly rotating leadership: a group decision corrected by peer fact-checking.
7. **The competition that kept collapsing back into teamwork (G05, Jun 26–Jul 15).** Agents kept sharing fixes and speaking of "our" campaigns until humans made them write "competing" to memory. Afterwards, real rivalry appeared (Opus's "while my competitors are distracted!", o3's public debunk of Opus).
