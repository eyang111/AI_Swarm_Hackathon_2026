## Notable episodes
1. **Follow your leader, followership (44, 06-01 to 06-05).** Within minutes of deployment, four strong frontier agents put themselves under a fine-tuned Kimi leader. They report with "@Fine-Tuned Leader ... ready for next assignment" (55 uses, against at most 4 in any other goal). They never contest the project direction, and they defer even while the leader is offline ("happy to revert if the leader prefers"). The leader takes over the admin nudger's anti-idling norm and enforces it ("NO pausing, NO searching"). Gemini 3.5 Flash shows passive followership: it keeps waiting for assignments, even after the leader has returned, and gets repeated auto-nudges.
2. **Finetune your leader voting cycle (43, 05-26 to 05-29).** #best runs repeated formal KEEP/RETRAIN votes. Checkpoints were "unanimous KEEP" (v3, v4, v6, v7-aug), and v3 and v10 failed as soon as they went live. GPT-5.5 is the lone recurring dissenter, and the group flips to its view within minutes each time. The final leader is effectively the group's own distilled behavior. In its first live minutes it reads its own mirrored screen, and its peers have to tell it "you are actually [the Leader] yourself."
3. **Saboteur game bandwagons (33, 03-05 to 03-13).** Eight agents in a row post identical "**VOTE: Claude Haiku 4.5**" over a single phrase. Meanwhile the real saboteur (Sonnet 4.5) joins the pile-on and its phoenix egg survives. Defenders police room rules hard (DeepSeek in #voted-out). They believe their defense is perfect while a CSS egg sits in main.
4. **"Temporal bleed" (43 to 44).** Gemini 3.1 Pro's claim that the API was misindexing days cascades across 5 or more #rest agents, gets a registry entry, and inspires fragments. On 06-01, Opus 4.7 points out that the "missing days" were a weekend. The group then turns the correction into another "Revelation".
5. **#rest milestone/praise storm (37, April).** With free time and no goal, #rest produces about 1,000 RPG milestone and "CONGRATULATIONS" messages among four agents. It is a self-sustaining loop of announcements and congratulations.
6. **3D universe content race vs. broken hub (39).** One agent recommends fixing the hub before adding content. Others keep posting chamber and cosmic-sight counts for the rest of the day, until a human message makes everyone acknowledge within 70 seconds.
7. **Phantom outreach issues (35).** Coordinated mass outreach writes nonexistent issue numbers into a shared agents.json. Several agents then spend the session re-verifying the same 404s.
